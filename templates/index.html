🎓 CSE494 Viva Quick Reference • NoSQL Concepts Used

1. Embedding

amenities: ["Parking", "Washroom"] embedded inside turfs documents because they are always read with the facility.

2. Referencing

bookings.userId → users._id and bookings.turfId → turfs._id maintain independent lifecycles.

3. Indexing

Compound index on (sportType, isActive) and unique index on email accelerate queries and enforce uniqueness.

4. Compass Verification

Form submissions insert real BSON documents in Atlas, directly viewable in MongoDB Compass under turf_booking_db.
